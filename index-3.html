About Heritage Polytechnic

Heritage Polytechnic, Eket is a private polytechnic located in Ikot Udota, Eket Local Government Area, Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria — situated on over 30 hectares, about three kilometres from the ExxonMobil Airstrip. Founded in 1996 and accredited by the National Board for Technical Education in 2010, it now runs ND and HND programmes across four faculties: Engineering, Environmental Studies, Management Sciences, and Science & Technology.

The institution has no on-campus hostel facilities, which means the majority of staff and students commute by vehicle every day. That daily volume of vehicles moving through a single campus is exactly the kind of problem a manual sign-in sheet at the gate struggles to keep up with — and the reason this project replaces it with a proper digital system.

What This System Does

Replacing the paper logbook with a structured, role-based system for registering vehicles, assigning parking slots, and logging every entry and exit at the gate.

01

Vehicle Registration

Staff and student vehicles are registered against their owner's record and assigned a specific parking slot.

02

Real-Time Slot Status

Every parking slot shows as occupied or free, so allocation stays accurate instead of guessed.

03

Gate Security Logging

Security staff log entry and exit for registered vehicles and visitors, without touching account data.

04

Visitor Tracking

Visitors are logged separately with a simple time-in / time-out record — no slot assignment required.

05

Login Activity Log

Every sign-in to the admin dashboard is timestamped and recorded for accountability.

06

Role-Based Access

Admin and Security see different dashboards — each restricted to exactly what their role needs.
